Fit & terms

A good fit

An asset-based carrier already running a TMS, fuel cards, or telematics with real history. If you are logging your loads, rates, and miles, we have something to work with. Some mess is fine, and we check a sample before you pay. Everything is delivered remotely.

No usable data yet?

No TMS, or records too thin for a 90-day read? Then there is nothing to audit yet, so I will lend you the Desk instead. The founding cohort is limited to 5 to 10 carriers, up to 10 trucks each, approved on a call. I load your trucks and drivers, you run the Desk free for 30 days with Signals included, and at day 30 you get a leak summary from the loads you ran. Then you stay on at $349 a month or export your data free.

The terms

The Read: $750 flat, about a week turnaround, credited toward your first 3 months of Signals or the Desk. It covers one TMS, one export and the last 90 days; anything beyond that is quoted separately. We check a sample of your export before you pay, and refund you in full if the data cannot support a read. The Desk: $99 + $25 a truck a month, $349 minimum, no Read required. Signals: quoted after the Read, and as current as the exports you send. Both are the same system for every carrier, so there is nothing custom to build or maintain. Founding cohort: 30 days free, application only, no card up front. Then $349 a month (the $99 + $25 a truck formula at 10 trucks, Signals included) only if you say yes, or export your data free. No hourly billing, no surprises.
