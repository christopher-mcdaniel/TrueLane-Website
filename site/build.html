05 · Questions
Is it live?

Yes, in early access. I onboard each carrier personally: your trucks, drivers, customers and alerts set up with you. The walkthrough above runs on a sample fleet, so no customer data is shown.

Is this only for dispatchers?

No. Dispatch is one room. Quoting, billing, driver pay, fuel, ELD, trailers and the audit trail share one record, so the numbers match everywhere and a leak in one place shows up in all of them.

What does it cost?

The Desk is $99 plus $25 a truck a month, with a $349 minimum (10 trucks is $349, 25 trucks is $724). It includes Signals and the driver app, and no Read is required. Signals on its own, for carriers who keep their TMS, is founding-carrier pricing quoted after the Leak Read, which is $750 and credited toward your first 3 months of Signals or the Desk. No card and no contract to start.

I dispatch for several carriers. Can I use it?

Yes. Each carrier runs as its own terminal in one desk, so trucks, drivers and loads stay separate, and invoices and driver statements carry that carrier’s own MC, DOT and remit-to. Payment goes to the carrier, not through the desk.

What counts as a read?

A read is one rate confirmation or dispatch screenshot read by an AI service and turned into a draft load. Each truck includes 25 a month, pooled across your desk. Extra reads are 25 cents each, billed separately, with a monthly limit so a busy month never surprises you. A read that fails is free.

How big a fleet can it handle?

It has been tested on 1,500 tractors, 5,000 trailers and 50,000 loads of invented data, with most pages opening in about a second.

Do I have to leave my TMS?

No. Start with the Leak Read and Signals alongside the system you run today. The desk is for when you want dispatch, billing and driver pay in one place.

Does it connect to my drivers’ phones, ELD, fuel cards or factoring company?

Yes. Driver phones, ELD and telematics, fuel cards and factoring connect to the desk. I confirm which connections are live for your providers before you sign, and if you use one that is not connected yet, tell me and it goes on the list.

How does this fit with the Leak Read and Signals?

The Leak Read finds where your money leaks, Signals alerts you when the next leak starts, and the desk is where you run the day. Start anywhere. The $750 is credited if you go further.
