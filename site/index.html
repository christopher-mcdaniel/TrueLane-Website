02 · App

Offer to invoice, in one app.

Your costsSetting your truck's costs once: the four steps. 15 to 25 seconds.File: media/web-costs.mp4

Your costs

Four short steps turn your truck’s bills into your cost per mile: monthly bills, per-mile costs, how you get paid and your targets. Set once.

CalculateAn offer goes in and the verdict comes out, with the basis line. 10 to 20 seconds.File: media/web-calculate.mp4

Calculate

Put in the offer and the route. Get a plain verdict: below your cost, at market or above, with your net after fuel and what a load home would change.

Rate con in. Load out.

Skip the typing. TrueLane reads your rate con and fills in the load: broker, stops, dates, equipment, pay terms.

Trip

Next stop, one tap: arrived, loaded, delivered. A dock clock times every wait, you snap a proof-of-delivery photo at each stop, and it keeps working with no signal.

InvoiceThe wrap-up chain to the invoice and Share. 15 to 25 seconds.File: media/web-invoice.mp4

Invoice

Finish the trip and the invoice is ready to review: your details, payment terms and delivery photos. Share the PDF and confirm it went out. Reminders at 5 and 15 days.

Taxes & recordsTax info, the year’s totals, the CSV and the audit bundle. 20 to 30 seconds.File: media/web-taxes.mp4

Taxes & records

Your year in one place: revenue, miles and tracked expenses, by actual expenses or standard mileage. Export a CSV, or an audit bundle with your receipts and delivery photos.

Insights & analyticsThe trip scorecard and All trip numbers, then Analytics. 20 to 30 seconds.File: media/web-scorecard.mp4

Insights & analytics

Every trip scored: profit per mile, net per hour, pay minus costs. Then the bigger picture: revenue by month, top lanes and brokers, how you price against market, and your Road Record.
