Privacy Policy

Effective date: [ADD DATE] · Superior One Logistics Corp.

TrueLane is a freight and logistics data analytics practice. Careful data handling is the whole point of what we do, so we keep this policy plain and specific: here is what we collect, why, and how we protect it.

Who we are

This site and service are operated by Superior One Logistics Corp. ("TrueLane," "we," "us"). You can reach us any time at superior.one.logistics@gmail.com.

Information you give us through the site

When you fill out the intake form, you choose to send us:

  • Your name, company, email, and (optionally) phone number.
  • The systems you run and a description of your data problem.
  • Your preferred time for a call, and whether you are interested in the Data Readiness Audit.

We use this information for one purpose: to prepare for and hold a conversation about whether we can help you, and to follow up about it. We do not use it for anything else.

Please do not send confidential operational data or credentials through the form. Real data waits until there is an engagement and, on request, an NDA in place.

How the form is processed

The intake form is delivered to us through a third-party form service (Web3Forms), which transmits your submission to our email. Your submission is subject to that provider's handling in transit. We do not store site submissions in any public database, and we do not sell or rent them.

Client data during an engagement

If we work together, we may be granted access to systems such as your TMS, load boards, fuel cards, telematics, or accounting data. That access is handled under strict practices:

  • Least privilege. Read-only access wherever the work allows, scoped service accounts over full logins, and access only to the systems in scope.
  • Credentials protected. Any credentials are stored in a password manager with multi-factor authentication, never in plaintext, email, or a spreadsheet.
  • Revocable and logged. You can revoke access at any time, and access is documented so it can be cleanly rotated or handed back.
  • Never sold or shared. Your operational data is used only to deliver your project. It is never sold, rented, or shared with third parties, and aggregated benchmarks are never traced back to you.

Specific handling, retention, and confidentiality terms are set in the written scope and any NDA for your engagement, which govern if they differ from this page.

Cookies and analytics

This site is a single static page and does not set advertising or tracking cookies of its own. It loads a web font from Google Fonts, which may receive your browser's request to serve that font. If we add analytics in the future, we will update this policy first.

How long we keep things

We keep intake submissions only as long as needed to follow up and, if we work together, to maintain our business records. You can ask us to delete your intake information at any time by emailing superior.one.logistics@gmail.com.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, correct it, or ask us to delete it. Just email superior.one.logistics@gmail.com and we will handle it promptly.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page and revise the effective date above.

Questions about privacy or your data? Email superior.one.logistics@gmail.com.
